Featured project · 01

GuardMo — Campus Lost & Found

A campus lost-and-found platform designed to help students report missing belongings and follow their reports, while giving school guards a way to manage recovered items.

Project showcase

Problem & purpose

Lost belongings on campus can be difficult to report and recover. GuardMo proposes a central place for students to submit item reports and keep track of their status.

Key features shown

  • Separate student and guard experiences
  • Lost and found reporting options
  • Student report list with status labels
  • Item details including image and location

My contribution

Project concept development, feature planning, interface direction, and AI-assisted development using Lovable.
